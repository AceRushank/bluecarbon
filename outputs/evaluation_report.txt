-- Plain-English Summary ----------------------------------------
  The Random Forest pipeline achieves an R2 of 0.9627 on the
  held-out test set, explaining 96.3% of the variance in total
  mangrove carbon stock across 720 global typological units. The
  typical prediction error is 67 MgC (246 tCO2e) per unit,
  representing 21.5% of the mean test-set carbon stock. 5-fold
  cross-validation gives a mean R2 of 0.9689 +/- 0.0046,
  confirming robustness across folds. AGB-related features
  dominate (together >85% importance), reflecting that
  aboveground biomass is both the primary carbon pool and a
  strong proxy for unit size, which in turn drives SOC stocks.
